Privacy Policy for Zen Tile Match 3D

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

Developer: Zen Tile Match Studios

Contact: support@zentilematch.com

Summary of Data Practices

Zen Tile Match 3D is designed with privacy-first architecture. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we track users across third-party websites or services.

1. Information We Collect

When you play Zen Tile Match 3D, we may collect the following limited categories of information:

  • Local Game Progress: High scores, completed levels, star achievements, and inventory are stored locally on your device storage (HTML5 LocalStorage / IndexedDB).
  • Device & Performance Information: Screen resolution, operating system version, and general error telemetry to ensure optimal 60fps performance across Android devices.
  • In-App Purchase Information: Digital goods transactions processed through Google Play Billing are securely handled by Google Play. We do not receive, store, or have access to your credit card or payment credentials.

2. How We Use Information

We use information solely to:

  • Save your puzzle campaign progression and unlock themes.
  • Provide instant, secure reward credit for completed puzzle milestones.
  • Ensure anti-cheat verification for daily check-in rewards.
  • Diagnose crashes and optimize game responsiveness.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

All gameplay data is stored client-side on your device. You can erase all game data at any time by clearing your application cache in Android Settings > Apps > Zen Tile Match 3D > Storage > Clear Data.

4. Children's Privacy

Zen Tile Match 3D does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our content is rated Everyone (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone) and provides wholesome puzzle entertainment suitable for all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to comply with Google Play Developer Policy updates. Any changes will be posted on this URL.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your data, please contact our support team at support@zentilematch.com.
